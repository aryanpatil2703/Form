Biopharma deal making in 2025 and 2026

Where Pharma Is Investing for the Future of Medicine

The biopharma industry is entering a new era of strategic deal making, with companies making fewer but larger bets to secure future growth. This report explores the deals, therapeutic areas, technologies and global innovation hubs shaping the future of medicine.

Key Insights

  • Record deal values: Biopharma deal values reached $292.6B in 2025, up 27% from 2024.
  • Mega-partnerships are reshaping pipelines: Pharma companies are prioritizing larger, multi-program partnerships to access platforms and strengthen future pipelines.
  • China is becoming a major innovation hub: Mainland China accounted for 32% of global out-licensing transactions in H1 2025, up from just 5% in 2020.
  • AI is transforming drug discovery: Partnerships between pharma, technology companies and AI-focused biotechs are creating new approaches to drug discovery and development.
  • Oncology remains a priority: ADCs, bispecific antibodies and radiopharmaceuticals continue to attract significant strategic investment.
  • New opportunities beyond established franchises: Leading pharma companies are expanding into RNA therapeutics, immunology, metabolic disease, gene therapy and other emerging modalities.
